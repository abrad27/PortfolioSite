That curiosity eventually evolved into a passion for understanding how those systems are secured, defended, and protected against cyber threats. What started with building computers has grown into a commitment to defending them.

    I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance at Western Governors University, where I'm developing the knowledge and technical skills needed for a career in Security Operations (SOC), threat hunting, and Blue Team operations.

    Beyond my coursework, I actively develop practical cybersecurity skills with projects built around my virtual enterprise simulation environment. My environment includes Active Directory with simulated workstations, pfSense, Security Onion, Splunk, Kali Linux, Nessus, OpenVAS, and MITRE Caldera, allowing me to simulate real-world enterprise environments, perform vulnerability assessments, monitor security events, and practice incident detection and response.

    I enjoy taking on projects that challenge me to solve real security problems and strengthen my understanding of enterprise infrastructure. Every project in this portfolio reflects my commitment to continuous learning and my desire to grow into a well-rounded cybersecurity professional.
